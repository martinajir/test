Arrr! A Collection of Pirate Jokes
===================================

1. Why couldn't the pirate play cards? Because he was sitting on the deck!

2. What's a pirate's favorite letter? You'd think it's R, but it's the C!

3. Why do pirates not know the alphabet? Because they always get lost at C.

4. How much does a pirate pay for corn? A buccaneer!

5. What did the pirate say when he turned 80? Aye matey!

6. Why did the pirate go to the doctor? Because he was feeling a little green around the gills, arrr!

7. What do you call a pirate who skips class? Captain Hooky!

8. Why was the pirate captain such a bad singer? Because he could never find the right key... he always lost it at C!

9. What does a pirate order at a bar? A pint of "grog" and a hook to hang his coat on!

10. Why don't pirates take showers before they walk the plank? They just wash up on shore.

11. What's a pirate's favorite exercise? Plank-ing!

12. How does a pirate keep his pants up? With a buccaneer belt... or just a good hook!

13. What do you call a pirate with two eyes and two legs? A rookie.

14. Why did the pirate buy an eye patch? Because he needed cover for his "arrr"-ea.

15. What's a pirate's favorite type of music? Anything with a good hook!

16. Why do pirates make great singers? They can hit the high Cs!

17. What do you call a pirate droid? Arrr2-D2!

18. Why did the pirate cross the ocean? To get to the other tide!

19. What is a pirate's favorite fast food? Arrrby's!

20. How do pirates prefer to communicate? Aye to aye.
