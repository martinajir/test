# Jokes

A collection of programmer and general jokes.

1. Why do programmers prefer dark mode? Because light attracts bugs.
2. There are 10 types of people in the world: those who understand binary and those who don't.
3. Why do Java developers wear glasses? Because they don't C#.
4. A SQL query walks into a bar, walks up to two tables and asks: "Can I join you?"
5. Why did the developer go broke? Because he used up all his cache.
6. How many programmers does it take to change a light bulb? None, that's a hardware problem.
7. Why do programmers hate nature? It has too many bugs.
8. I told my computer I needed a break, and now it won't stop sending me KitKats.
9. Why was the JavaScript developer sad? Because he didn't Node how to Express himself.
10. There are only two hard things in computer science: cache invalidation, naming things, and off-by-one errors.
